Lámina: `docs/design/2026-11-09-logo-propuestas.png`. Creada con herramienta integrada ImageGen, como exploración raster. - **B Trazado (aprobada):** carretera sinuosa ascendente con punto de llegada. Conecta con rutas, desnivel y progresión y se usa como marca global.

- La aplicación usa sus tintas actuales (`--ink`, `--ink-3`) y el azul de trazado (`--trace`), con adaptación automática a los temas oscuro/claro. ## Prompt de generación

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: one professional logo exploration presentation board for FBikeTrain, a premium personal road-cycling app with a cartographic visual identity. Primary request: THREE clearly different original logo proposals replacing an existing unsatisfactory letter F icon. Do NOT use an F monogram, milestone F, or generic bicycle stock pictogram. These are new design proposals, not an implementation or final vector files. Create a beautifully aligned landscape brand-design board, warm ivory background, three equally wide columns separated by hairline sage rules, generous whitespace, very crisp vector-like edges. At top set the accurate word 'FBikeTrain' with a small subtitle 'Tres caminos. Una identidad.' Each column has a letter and short concept title, one large primary symbol, the symbol beside a clean bold slightly condensed sans wordmark spelled exactly 'FBikeTrain', a small one-color forest-green version, and a very small favicon/application-icon example to demonstrate readability. Make the large symbols genuinely simple, reproducible and legible at 24px. Use approximately dark forest #173D33, sage #D8DFC5, warm ivory #F5F1E6, cobalt accent #2465D5. No gradients, no drop shadows, no photoreal objects, no 3D, no mockups on merchandise, no decorative scenery. Consistent optical weight and comparable footprint across all 3 proposals. Proposal A, label 'A · Cumbre': a refined geometric mountain/contour mark, two or three nested softly angular ascent contours forming a mountain silhouette, a subtle small blue route summit marker. This should feel like a distinctive cartographic emblem, not a generic triangular mountain drawing. Proposal B, label 'B · Trazado': an elegant single bold continuous S-shaped road hairpin ascending diagonally with a clear blue route endpoint. A subtle abstract B can emerge from the two bends, but it must remain a clean compact route symbol, not a literal text letter or infinite loop. Express cycling movement through route geometry, no arrows. Proposal C, label 'C · Cadencia': a precise circular cycling wheel emblem with one gently ascending topographic/elevation line crossing its interior and a restrained blue accent segment. Avoid many spokes, tiny lines, complex gear teeth or literal cyclist illustrations. Each concept should be unique, ownable-looking and fit an editorial cartography-inspired interface. In each column the one-color sample and tiny icon must accurately match the main mark. Do not add lengthy explanation text or extra unrequested logos. Make this look like a high-end graphic designer's first-round brand presentation, with sharp Spanish labels and excellent proportion.
